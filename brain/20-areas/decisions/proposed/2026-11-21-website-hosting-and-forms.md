# Decision: website hosting and inquiry form delivery

## Context
The sample is not published, and its form sends nothing. The live site needs hosting, HTTPS, a domain, and a way for inquiries to reach Salig staff with spam protection and a privacy notice. See [[existing-site-review]] and [[website-completion]].

## Options
1. Netlify, with Netlify Forms.
2. Cloudflare Pages, with a small form handler sending email.
3. Vercel, with a form or email service.
4. Another host Aldred already uses.

## Recommendation
No recommendation yet. `product-engineer` should compare cost, form handling, data location, spam protection and ease of use once the tech approach is decided, then this decision is updated with a recommendation.

## Needed from Aldred
Nothing yet. Aldred will be asked to choose after the comparison. Also needed later: the domain name, and which inbox receives inquiries.
