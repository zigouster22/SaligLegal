# Decision: brand name and legal entities shown on the website

## Context
The existing sample uses "Salig Legal Operations" and describes "a Philippine corporation and a U.S. LLC". The company overview uses "Salig Legal" and describes a California law firm with a Philippine sister company. The approved audience is California lawyers and law firms that need legal assistants ([[2026-11-21-website-audience-california-law-firms]]). See [[existing-site-review]] and [[website-completion]].

## Options
1. "Salig Legal Operations", naming the Philippine corporation and U.S. LLC in the footer and About page. Matches the logo and sample; signals a support company rather than a law firm.
2. "Salig Legal". Shorter, but could read as a law firm offering legal services, which the site does not do.
3. Another name Aldred chooses.

## Recommendation
Option 1, subject to a California lawyer confirming the name does not imply the company is a law firm or practises law. The exact entity names need to come from Aldred.

## Needed from Aldred
Which brand name the site uses, and the exact legal entity names (and states/countries of registration) to show.

## Outcome
Approved by Aldred on 2026-11-21: option 1, the site uses **Salig Legal Operations**. Still open: the exact legal entity names and registration details (Aldred to supply), and a California lawyer's confirmation that the name does not imply a law firm.
