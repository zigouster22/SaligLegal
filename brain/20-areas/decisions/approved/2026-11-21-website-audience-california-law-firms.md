# Decision: the website's audience is California lawyers and law firms that need legal assistants

## Context
The existing site sample (see [[existing-site-review]]) markets "Salig Legal Operations", which sells remote legal administrative and attorney-directed casework teams to law firms. The company overview described Salig Legal as a California law firm. Which business the website is for decides the audience, the California rules that apply and the pages needed.

## Options
1. (a) The outsourcing company, selling support teams to law firms.
2. (b) A California law firm serving the public.
3. (c) Both, as separate sites or sections.

## Recommendation
Option (a): the sample, brand and logo are built for it.

## Needed from Aldred
Which business and audience the public website serves.

## Outcome
Approved by Aldred on 2026-11-21: the audience is **California lawyers and law firms that need legal assistants**. The website markets the outsourcing services (option a). It is not a site offering legal services to the public.

Consequences:
- Site copy is written for lawyers and firms as buyers, not for consumers seeking legal help.
- Compliance review focuses on California unauthorized-practice limits for the support company, client firms' supervision and confidentiality duties, and privacy for the inquiry form. Attorney-advertising rules for the firm's own services do not apply to this site unless Aldred decides otherwise (lawyer to confirm).
- Brand name, code location, tech approach and hosting remain open; see `brain/20-areas/decisions/proposed/`.
