# 20 Areas

Ongoing responsibilities with a standard to keep up but no finish line. Areas (others created as needed):

- `decisions/`: the decision log, split into `proposed/` (awaiting Aldred) and `approved/` (approved by Aldred). See its README.
- `brand-voice/`: tone, terminology and messaging for Salig Legal
- `seo/`: keywords, page metadata, search performance notes
- `compliance/`: advertising rules for legal services, disclaimers, privacy and cookie obligations
- `site-operations/`: hosting, analytics, forms, maintenance
